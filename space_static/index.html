Phonemic or graphemic input?

Devanagari does not write schwa deletion. नमक is spoken namak, not namaka, and nothing in the spelling says so. One arm below is handed the deletion by a rule-based front end. The other reads the characters and has to work it out. Everything else about the two is the same: architecture, seed, corpus, step budget.

Each sentence carries the natural recording as well, so the question can be how far each arm is from the speaker rather than only which arm you prefer. Sentences are from the held-out split, which no model trained on, and the highlighted words are the ones where medial schwa deletion applies.
