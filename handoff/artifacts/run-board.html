Does कमल still need a G2P?

Whether explicit Devanagari grapheme-to-phoneme conversion still buys anything for neural TTS, or whether the architecture absorbs Hindi schwa deletion from data alone. Hindi is the testbed, Marathi the same-script control, a 9 h to 10 min ladder varies resource level, and every run is held to one budget: 100,000 steps at 12,000 mel frames in bf16 on one A100-40GB.

The thing being measured: कमल is /kəməl/ but कमलों is /kəmloː/ — the second schwa drops. 39/47 of the rule's calls match native-speaker judgement.
